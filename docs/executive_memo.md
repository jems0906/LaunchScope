# Executive Memo (Template)

**Initiative:** Fictional game-discovery module offer

**Decision owner:** Content & Games Product Lead

**Plan version / analysis reference:** To be recorded when a frozen plan is analyzed

## Question and design

Among households eligible before assignment, what is the effect of offering the module, versus the existing experience, on the chance of at least one qualified game session within 28 days? Household-level randomized assignment supports an intention-to-treat comparison if allocation and outcome capture remain valid.

## Evidence

Report treatment and control assigned counts, observed counts, outcome rates, absolute difference, uncertainty interval, p-value, practical threshold, and the playback-quality and stability guardrail differences and intervals. Report exposure, interaction, repeat engagement, and completeness with denominators. Do not substitute post-assignment clickers for the assigned population.

## Interpretation and limitations

The result concerns the offer's effect on the specified session behavior. It does not establish satisfaction, retention, financial value, or creative quality. Describe missingness, spillover, measurement validity, power, and any deviation from the frozen plan. Nonsignificant guardrails do not establish absence of harm.

## Recommendation

Choose: continue / redesign / collect more evidence / stop / inconclusive / this design cannot answer the question. State stakeholder judgment and operational considerations separately from statistical evidence. There is no automatic release decision or composite success score.
