# LaunchScope Portfolio Review

**Demo:** https://frontend-production-2b4c.up.railway.app/

Allow 5–7 minutes. Use the public read-only demo; do not sign in or enter real or sensitive data. Ask the reviewer to think aloud and avoid explaining the page until they have answered the tasks.

## Tasks

1. In your own words, what decision is this study meant to inform? Identify the treatment, control, population, primary outcome, and time horizon.
2. Explore the **Tradeoff** scenario. What does the primary result suggest? What do the playback-quality and stability guardrails suggest? What would you recommend, and why?
3. What can this study not establish? Find the interpretation boundary and explain it in your own words.
4. Find the subgroup note. What does V1 claim about subgroups, and what would an analyst need to do before interpreting a subgroup result?

## Questions

- How clear was the decision question? (1 = unclear, 5 = very clear)
- How clear were the distinction between the primary outcome and guardrails, and the practical harm margin? (1–5)
- Did any result or label seem to imply more certainty than the evidence supports? Which one?
- At what point, if any, did you feel unsure what to do or what a number meant?
- What one change would make the evidence easier to use in a real decision discussion?

Record answers without names or sensitive information. Treat feedback as usability evidence, not validation of the statistical methodology or of any real-world outcome.
